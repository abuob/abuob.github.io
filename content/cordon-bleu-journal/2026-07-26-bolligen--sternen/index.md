The third visit to the Sternen - the other visits didn't make it into the blog, primarily because of not finding the time.
They have very generous portions for the price one pays!

This time, I took the "Cordon Bleu Greek Edition" from their Specialty Menu, and I have to say, I was very positively surprised!
The Greek cheese with its light sourness makes the Cordon Bleu taste quite a bit different from a more traditional choice of cheese,
but I certainly enjoyed it.

As the Sternen is also open on Sundays at lunchtime and is rather easy to reach from my place,
I'll very likely visit again, and will certainly consider the Greek cheese version again!

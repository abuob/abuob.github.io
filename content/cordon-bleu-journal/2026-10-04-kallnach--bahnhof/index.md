The second visit in the Bahnhöfli in Kallnach (the first visit isn't registered in this blog); their menu has a crazy amount of choices, with some quite adventurous entries.
The menu of the day was a special Cordon Bleu - made from wild boar, which wasn't something I've tasted before, so I went for it.

It was definitely an interesting experience, but I guess I'll stick to normal pork in the future.

Overall, the Bahnhöfli is a fantastic address for Cordon Bleu enjoyers - and, since the "Jura" in Kerzers closed quite recently,
probably the uncontested best address in the area for cordon bleus.

Not what I'd typically expect when ordering a Cordon Bleu but perfectly sized for an evening.
Fantastic breading. The raclette-cheese was almost a bit too dominant for my liking, but that's a matter of preference.

Small letdown in an otherwise solid experience: The fries could've been a bit crisper.

On another note, if I visit this restaurant another time, I'll probably take (when available) their Asparagus Ravioli.
I got to try some from my SO, and they were absolutely out of this world.

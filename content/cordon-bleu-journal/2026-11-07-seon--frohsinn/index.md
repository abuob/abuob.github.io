The Frohsinn got crowned "Best Cordon Bleu 2026" by the Blick - not exactly my kind of journal, so my colleague and I had to verify ourselves.
We weren't let down! Definitely an address for a great Cordon Bleu.

As I'm becoming increasingly fond of soft cheeses in Cordon Bleus, I chose the "Fribourg"-version filled with Vacherin.
They serve portions only in one size - and they certainly make it hard to go home hungry.

To be honest, I'm not sufficiently into food-critiquing to determine whether this was really "the best" Cordon Bleu with the same certainty as the Blick does.
But among the restaurants I know, the Frohsinn is definitely one of the better addresses when it comes to Cordon Bleus - and certainly worth a visit!
